## FAQ (also inside the description)

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No, for using it: you install it once (copy-paste commands, or Docker) and then everything happens in the dashboard. Yes, if you want to change the code itself. It's clean TypeScript with a developer guide.

**Are there monthly fees?**
There are no platform fees to us. You pay your own hosting (typically $6–12/month for all your clients) and AI usage directly to Anthropic or OpenAI. The dashboard shows estimated AI cost per client.

**Which AI models does it support?**
Anthropic Claude is built in (Claude Opus 5 by default; Sonnet 5 and Haiku 4.5 are one click away). Any OpenAI-compatible API also works: OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Together, Mistral, DeepSeek or local Ollama. There's also a demo mode that needs no key.

**Can I use it for unlimited clients?**
Yes. Host bots for as many clients as you like and charge them whatever you want. You can't resell or share the source code itself.

**Is it really white-label?**
Yes. Your agency name, logo and colors appear on the dashboard, client portal, lead emails and the widget's "Powered by" link, which you can also hide per bot.

**Will it work on my clients' websites?**
Yes, on any site that allows a custom script: WordPress, Webflow, Wix (premium), Squarespace (Business), Shopify, GoHighLevel and custom sites. There's also a hosted chat page link for sites where you can't add code.

**What's the difference between Agency and Agency Pro?**
Same software and license. Pro adds all 12 industry templates (vs. 3) and priority support.

**Do I get updates?**
Yes, 12 months of updates are included.
