I can't create these accounts for you. They need your identity, email and phone verification, payout details and the Etsy setup-fee payment, and the marketplaces require the account owner to create them. Everything else is ready to upload.

## 1. Etsy shop (20–30 min)

- [ ] Go to **etsy.com/sell** → *Get started*. Use a dedicated email (e.g. `amemsheets@…`).
- [ ] Shop preferences: language, country, currency (USD is fine for an international audience).
- [ ] Shop name. Ideas (check availability): **AmemSheets**, **AmemPlanners**, **SheetsByAmem**, **CalmMoneySheets**. Update `[YOUR SHOP NAME]` in the listing descriptions to match.
- [ ] Create the 3 listings using `listings/ETSY_LISTINGS.md` (title, tags, description, price, files, photos, video).
- [ ] Payments: Etsy Payments (bank account or Payoneer, depending on your country) and identity verification.
- [ ] Billing: add a card for the one-time setup fee ($15–29 depending on region) and the $0.20 listing fees.
- [ ] Shop icon: crop `images/marketing/pin-01-budget-planner.jpg` to a square, or use your own logo. Banner: `images/listing/gumroad-cover.jpg`.
- [ ] About, policies and FAQ: paste from the listing file.
- [ ] **Marketing → Sales and discounts → Promo code `LAUNCH25`** (25%, 7 days).
- [ ] **Marketing → Etsy Ads:** $1–3/day, all listings.

## Rules to stay safe

- Only you log in to your accounts. Never share passwords or 2FA codes with anyone, including AI tools.
- Keep the **AI disclosure** in every Etsy listing.
- No fake reviews, no buying reviews, no "only 2 left" style fake scarcity on a digital product.
- Taxes: you're responsible for declaring this income where you live.
