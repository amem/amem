# 24-hour launch plan

**Goal:** first sale within 24 hours. **Honest odds:** possible, not guaranteed. New Etsy shops usually take 1–8 weeks for a first organic sale. The fastest sales come from people who already know you, sent to a direct link with a launch code. Etsy and Pinterest keep selling after that.

**What's already done:** product files (built and checked), 13 listing images, 3 listing videos, 6 social images, all listing text, all post text.

---

## Hour 0–1: set up (see `ACCOUNT_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md`)
- [ ] Test the DEMO file in Google Sheets and Excel (10 min)
- [ ] Payhip account + 3 products + `LAUNCH25` coupon → **you now have a working `[LINK]`**

## Hour 1–2: Etsy
- [ ] Open the Etsy shop, publish all 3 listings (hero first), add videos
- [ ] Create promo code `LAUNCH25`, turn on Etsy Ads at $1–3/day

## Hour 2–4: warm network (the most important part)
- [ ] Send **20–30 personal messages** (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Messenger) using the template in `marketing/SOCIAL_POSTS.md` §1. Personal means using their name, one message at a time.
- [ ] Post the 4-slide Instagram/WhatsApp story
- [ ] Publish Instagram post 1 (`ig-01-launch.jpg`)
- [ ] Publish the LinkedIn post

## Hour 4–8: wider reach
- [ ] Pinterest: create 2 boards and pin all 4 pins, linking to the Etsy listings (or Payhip)
- [ ] Join 3–5 Facebook budgeting or Christmas-planning groups; post only in promo threads
- [ ] Post in r/SideProject (follow its rules)
- [ ] Record the 15-second Reel/TikTok (script in §2 of the social file) and post it

## Hour 8–24: follow up
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM within the hour
- [ ] Publish Instagram post 2 (`ig-02-holiday.jpg`) in the evening; many people shop for gifts at night
- [ ] Ask the first buyer (a friend is fine) for honest feedback and, if they liked it, an honest review on Etsy
- [ ] Log the numbers in `sales-tracker.csv`

---

## Days 2–7: keep it moving
- One new pin per day on Pinterest; one story per day on Instagram.
- **Day 3 check (Etsy Stats):**
  - Views but no favorites → change the first photo (try `etsy-bundle-03` as the thumbnail).
  - Favorites but no sales → test $9.99 for the bundle.
  - No views → add $2/day to ads and check your tags match what buyers search.
- **Day 7:** `LAUNCH25` ends. Post "last day of launch price".

## Weeks 2–8: grow
- Add listings from the same engine (each new edition is a few lines in `product/src/build_workbook.py`): "Couples budget", "Paycheck budget", "Wedding budget", "Small business income & expense tracker".
- January is the peak for budget searches. Make sure listings have reviews and a strong photo by late December.
- Next product: 2027 digital planner (GoodNotes PDF), the runner-up in the research.

## Numbers to watch
| Metric | Healthy early range | What to do if it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy click-through (Stats) | 1–3% | Change the thumbnail photo |
| Etsy conversion (orders ÷ visits) | 1–3% | Improve photos 2–4, review the price |
| Payhip conversion from warm traffic | 3–10% | Make the message more personal; use the video |
