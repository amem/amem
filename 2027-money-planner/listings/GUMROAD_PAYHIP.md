# Direct store: Gumroad or Payhip

Etsy brings buyers who are searching. A direct store gives you **one link** for Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Pinterest. It's live in about 15 minutes, with no setup fee.

**Which one?**
- **Payhip (free plan):** 5% fee plus PayPal/Stripe processing. It handles EU VAT, supports coupon codes, and both PayPal and Stripe can pay out. **Recommended.**
- **Gumroad:** 10% + $0.50 per sale (30% if the buyer finds you through Gumroad Discover). It acts as merchant of record and handles sales tax for you. Simple, but the most expensive.

Check which payout methods your country supports before you choose. Stripe and PayPal availability varies by country.
