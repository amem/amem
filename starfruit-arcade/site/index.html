Questions clients ask

Do players need to install anything?

No. The games run in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. A link or a QR code is all a player needs.

Can the game live on my own website?

Yes. Upload the folder to any web host, or embed the game with one iframe tag. Every order includes help with this step.

Who owns the game you make for me?

You receive the full source code and the right to use, change and publish it for your project.

Can the game show ads or collect emails?

Yes. Every game has hooks for web-game ad networks, and Promo Arcade includes an optional sign-up form that can send entries to your spreadsheet or mailing tool.

How fast is delivery?

Rebrands take about 3 days, custom games 1 to 2 weeks, and most fixes 1 to 3 days.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo, brand colours, product pictures or names, and what the game should achieve, for example more newsletter sign-ups or visits to your shop.
