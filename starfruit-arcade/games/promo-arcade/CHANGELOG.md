# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — initial release

- Three brandable mini-games: Catch Rush, Memory Match, Spin & Win, plus a hub page.
- One-file rebrand (`brand.config.js`): logo, colours with automatic contrast, products,
  rewards and coupon codes, wheel odds, CTA, legal text, translations.
- Coupon card with copy button, optional lead-capture form with webhook, spins-per-day limit.
- 20 built-in flat product icons; custom images and emoji supported.
- Confetti, synthesized sounds, share button, analytics hooks, `?demo=1` mode.
