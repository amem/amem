# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — initial release

- 24 handcrafted levels: score, collect and jelly goals; shaped boards up to 9 × 9.
- Special pieces (line blaster, bomb, rainbow) and special + special combos.
- Cascades with rising chimes, praise banners, bonus points for moves left.
- 1–3 stars per level, level select, unlocks and best scores saved.
- Hints, automatic reshuffle, swipe / tap-tap / keyboard controls.
- Six drawn piece shapes with faces, or your own images.
- Difficulty and star scores tuned with simulated play.
- Title bot, `?demo=1` attract mode, pause, mute, share, ad / analytics hooks, translatable texts.
