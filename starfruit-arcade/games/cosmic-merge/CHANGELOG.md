# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — initial release

- Physics merge puzzle with 11 procedural planets, cute animated faces and a glass jar.
- Stable position-based circle physics with sub-steps, friction and rolling.
- Chain-reaction combos, discovery banners, BIG BANG black-hole finale.
- UFO dropper with aim guide, NEXT preview, evolution chain, overflow countdown.
- Responsive portrait / landscape layouts; title screen with a live bot; `?demo=1` mode.
- Pause, sound toggle, best score and best planet saved, share button.
- Monetization / analytics hooks, translatable texts, custom image support per tier.
