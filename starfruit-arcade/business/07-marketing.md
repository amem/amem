# 07 — Marketing: getting the first clients and players

## 1. Local and online businesses (your fastest first sale)

Business owners do not search for "HTML5 game". They buy when they **see their own brand in a
working game**. So show them.

**Who:** cafés, smoothie/juice bars, bakeries, restaurants, gyms, salons, boutiques, phone shops,
small online stores, event organisers — anyone with an Instagram account and a promotion to run.

**The 5-minute spec demo**
1. Copy `games/promo-arcade/` to `clients/<business>/`.
2. In `brand.config.js`: their name, colours (pick from their Instagram), 6–8 matching built-in
   icons (café → coffee, cupcake, donut, icecream; shop → bag, gift, shirt, heart, gem, star),
   prizes that fit them ("Free coffee", "10% off").
3. Open `spin-win/index.html` or `catch-rush/index.html`, record 15 seconds of play with your
   phone's screen recorder.
4. Send it **privately** (see templates). Do not publish a demo with their brand, and do not use
   their logo file without permission: the demo uses their name and colours only.

**Instagram / WhatsApp DM**
```text
Hi [name]! I made a quick game idea for [business] — a "Spin & Win" wheel in your colours where customers win [free cookie / 10% off] (15-sec video below).

It works from a link or a QR code at your counter, and can collect e-mails for your newsletter. No app needed.

Want me to set it up for you? This week it's $[price] all-in, live in 2 days.
```

**E-mail**
```text
Subject: A mini-game for [business]'s next promotion

Hi [name],

I build small promotional games for local businesses. I made a quick example in [business]'s colours: a prize wheel where customers win [prize] and get a code to use at checkout. (15-second video attached.)

Why it works:
• Customers play for 30 seconds and leave with a reason to buy
• Share it by link, QR code or on your website — no app
• Optional: collect e-mails for your newsletter

I can set it up with your logo, prizes and odds for $[price], ready in 2 days.

Would that be useful for your [season / opening / event]?

[Your name]
[Fiverr or website link]
```

**Follow-up (3–4 days later, once)**
```text
Hi [name], just checking whether you had a chance to watch the little game video. Happy to adapt the prizes or try a catch game with your products instead. No pressure either way!
```

**Pricing to quote directly:** $60–150 per game (see [09-pricing-and-growth.md](09-pricing-and-growth.md)).
Payment: if the client found you outside Fiverr/Upwork, use a payment link (PayPal, Payoneer,
Stripe, Wise) with 50 % upfront. Clients you met **on** Fiverr/Upwork must stay on that platform.

**Volume target:** 5 personalised messages per weekday. Expect most to stay unanswered; one yes
in 20–40 messages is a good start, and each delivered game becomes a portfolio piece.

## 2. Agencies (repeat work)

Marketing agencies run campaigns for many brands and pay well for a reliable white-label
developer.
```text
Hi [name], I build branded mini-games (prize wheels, catch and memory games) that agencies resell to their clients for campaigns and lead generation. Live demo: [link]. I deliver in 2–5 days under your agency's name, with source files. Could this fit any of your client campaigns this quarter?
```

## 4. Communities (read each community's rules first)

- **r/WebGames**, **r/playmygame**: post the free itch.io game with a short, honest description.
- **r/IndieDev**, **r/gamedev** (Screenshot Saturday threads): share a GIF and what you learned.
- **itch.io** devlogs and comments, **X/Twitter** #gamedev #indiedev, **LinkedIn** for the
  business side ("I built a prize-wheel game for a café: here is what happened").
- Give before you ask: comment helpfully on others' work. Self-promotion that only drops links
  gets removed.

## 5. Track what works

A spreadsheet with: date · channel · who · message used · reply? · call/order? · revenue.
Every two weeks, double the channel that produced replies and cut the one that did not.
