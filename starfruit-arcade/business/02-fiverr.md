# 02 — Fiverr: profile and 6 gigs

Fiverr is where small businesses search for "spin wheel", "branded game", "html5 game" and
"fix my game". Your edge: every gig shows a **finished, playable product**, and most orders
are a rebrand of a kit you already own, so you can deliver early.

Gig images are in `marketing/out/fiverr/` (1280 × 769). Trailers for the gig video are in
`marketing/out/video/` (MP4, about 20 s; Fiverr accepts up to about 75 s).

> **Before you publish:** read Fiverr's current Terms of Service and Community Standards.
> Keep all communication and payment on Fiverr, never share an email or phone number, and only
> share demo links as work samples if Fiverr's current rules allow it. See [10-rules-and-safety.md](10-rules-and-safety.md).

## 8. Ranking and conversion tips

- **Reply fast.** Response time and online status matter a lot for new sellers. Keep the app on.
- **Answer every inquiry with a mini-demo** when it is a business: a 5-minute rebrand of Spin &
  Win with their logo (see [08-order-fulfillment.md](08-order-fulfillment.md) §1), shown as a
  screenshot or short video in the Fiverr chat. Then send a custom offer.
- **Deliver early** and over-deliver a little (a QR code, a second colour option).
- **Check Briefs** (buyer requests matched to sellers) daily and answer only the ones you fit.
- **Do not** ask for or trade reviews, offer discounts for reviews, or take payment outside
  Fiverr. These get accounts banned.
- After 3–5 good reviews, raise prices about 20 % ([09-pricing-and-growth.md](09-pricing-and-growth.md)).
