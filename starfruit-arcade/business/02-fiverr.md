links to the same live demos. Start with **launch prices** (left column) until you have

## 6. Ranking and conversion checklist

- [ ] Answer every message within an hour while you are awake (use the mobile app).
- [ ] Check **Briefs** (Fiverr's buyer requests) every day and answer the relevant ones.
- [ ] Use the exact first sentence of a buyer's message in your reply; quote a fixed price.
- [ ] Offer a **custom offer** when a buyer's need sits between two packages.
- [ ] Deliver early, include a short Loom-style screen video of the result, and ask for honest
      feedback (never offer anything in exchange for a review).
- [ ] After 5 reviews, raise prices to the regular column; after 20, test +20 % again.
- [ ] Share each gig link on your social profiles (Fiverr counts outside traffic).
