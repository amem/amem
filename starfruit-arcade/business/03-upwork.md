# 03 — Upwork: profile, portfolio, projects and proposals

Upwork clients post jobs and choose from proposals. It rewards specific, fast, relevant
proposals, and it costs "Connects" to apply, so quality beats volume. Bigger budgets than
Fiverr, slower start.

> Check Upwork's current fee schedule, Connects pricing and Terms of Service before you start.
> Keep every conversation and payment on Upwork until a contract exists, and after.

---

## 4. Finding jobs

Saved searches (check them 2–3 times a day; early proposals get read):

- `html5 game` · `javascript game` · `phaser` (only if the job allows vanilla JS or you are confident)
- `spin wheel` · `prize wheel` · `gamification` · `interactive quiz` · `advergame`
- `canvas animation` · `browser game bug` · `game portal sdk` · `crazygames`
- `marketing game` · `lead generation game` · `instagram game`
- `advent calendar` · `scratch card` · `product quiz` · `shopify quiz` · `match 3` · `christmas campaign`

**Good signs:** payment verified, clear description, budget ≥ $50, client has hired before,
fewer than 20 proposals. **Skip:** "unpaid test", vague one-liners, "need it in 1 hour for $10",
anything asking you to move to Telegram/WhatsApp or to download and run unknown files.

## 5. Proposal rules

1. First line answers their problem in their words. No "Dear Sir/Madam", no life story.
2. Show proof: a **specific** demo that matches (link to the game page on your site, or a
   screenshot). For businesses, a 5-minute rebrand with their logo is gold.
3. Give a plan in 3 steps and a timeline.
4. Ask one smart question.
5. Under 150 words. Fixed price where possible.

## 6. Proposal templates

Replace everything in [brackets]. Keep them short and personal.

**A. Branded game / gamification for a business**
> Hi [name], a [prize wheel / catch game] in [business]'s colours is exactly what I build.
> Here is a working one you can play on your phone: [link to spin-win or catch-rush demo].
> I rebranded it with your logo in 5 minutes to show the idea: [screenshot].
>
> Plan: 1) you send prizes and codes, 2) preview link in 48 h, 3) final version with
> [lead capture to your Google Sheet / QR code / embed for your site].
> Fixed price: $[X], delivered in [N] days.
>
> Quick question: should players leave an email before they see their code, or after?

**B. Reskin / custom HTML5 game**
> Hi [name], I can build this as a fast HTML5 game that runs in any browser. I have a
> [merge puzzle / stacking / brick-breaker] engine that already matches 80 % of what you
> describe: [demo link]. Reskinning it to [theme] means a playable preview in 2 days instead of weeks.
> Plan: preview → your feedback → polish and delivery with full source code. $[X], [N] days.
> Do you already have graphics, or should I design them?

**C. Bug fix / performance**
> Hi [name], [problem in their words] is usually caused by [likely cause, e.g. the canvas not
> scaling for devicePixelRatio / the game loop not using delta time]. I can confirm in 30 minutes
> once I see the code. Fixed price: $[X]. You get the fixed files and a short note of what changed.
> Can you share the project as a zip or repo, and the device/browser where it breaks?

**D. Game-portal / ad SDK integration**
> Hi [name], I can integrate the [CrazyGames / GameDistribution / other] SDK: midgame ads on
> restart, rewarded ads where it makes sense, pause and mute during ads, and the portal's
> checklist items (no external links, proper resize). My own games already use these hooks.
> $[X], [N] days. Is the game already approved on the portal, or should I prepare it for submission?

**E. Interactive marketing (quiz, scratch card, calendar, etc.)**
> Hi [name], I build lightweight interactive pieces for campaigns that load fast on phones,
> with optional email capture and analytics events. See a similar build: [link to the
> Daily Doors calendar / Scratch & Win / Perfect Match Quiz demo].
> I would do [one-sentence approach], preview in [N] days. $[X].
> What should the player see or win at the end?

**F. Long-term / retainer**
> Hi [name], I enjoy long-term work: I can take care of [new levels / seasonal themes / fixes]
> every month with a fixed monthly scope. Recent work: [2 demo links].
> Proposed: [N] hours or [deliverables] per month for $[X], with a weekly update.
> What is the most important thing to ship in the first two weeks?

## 7. Daily Upwork routine (20–30 minutes)

1. Open saved searches, skim jobs from the last few hours.
2. Send 2 tailored proposals (template + 3 personal lines + a matching demo).
3. Reply to every message within hours.
4. Once a week: refresh the profile title and overview with what clients actually ask for.
