## 6. Proposal templates

Rules that win: answer in the first hours after the job is posted; open with *their* goal in
*their* words; prove it with one relevant demo; give a concrete plan, a timeline and a price; end
with a question. Keep it under 150 words. Replace everything in [brackets].

### A. Marketing game / spin wheel / gamification
```text
Hi [name], a [spin-to-win wheel / catch game] in your brand is exactly what I build.

You can play a working example in my portfolio ("Promo Arcade"): a prize wheel, catch game and memory game with coupon codes and an optional e-mail form.

For [business], I would:
1. Put your logo, colours and [products] into the game
2. Set your prizes and odds, with one-tap coupon copy
3. Send sign-ups to [Google Sheets / Mailchimp / your API]
4. Help you put it on [your site / a QR code]

Preview link in 2 days, finished in [3–5] days, fixed price $[X].

Should players be limited to one spin per day, or can they replay?
```

### B. Custom HTML5 / hyper-casual game
```text
Hi [name], I read your brief for [game idea]. The core loop ([one-sentence loop]) is a great fit for HTML5: instant loading, works on any phone.

Relevant demo in my portfolio: [Cosmic Merge / Brick Blitz / Neon Stack], which has the same kind of [physics / levels / one-tap] gameplay.

Plan:
1. Day 1–2: playable prototype of the core mechanic (link you can test on your phone)
2. Then: art direction, menus, sound, scoring, [levels / power-ups]
3. Final: polish, mobile testing, source code + short guide

Timeline [N] days, fixed price $[X]. Where will the game be published: your website, a portal or an app web view?
```

### C. Bug fix / performance / mobile issues
```text
Hi [name], [the issue in their words] is something I fix often in canvas games. Usually it comes from [frame-rate dependent movement / canvas sizing / touch events / timers that keep running].

If you can share the project (zip or repo access), I will:
1. Reproduce the problem on phone and desktop
2. Fix it and explain the cause in a short report
3. Re-test everything that could be affected

Most fixes like this take 1–2 days. Fixed price $[X], and I will confirm after a first look.

Which browsers/devices show the problem?
```

### D. Ads / portal integration (CrazyGames, GameDistribution, etc.)
```text
Hi [name], I can integrate [portal]'s SDK into your game and make it pass their technical review.

What I would do:
1. Add the SDK, gameplay start/stop events, and midgame / rewarded ads at natural breaks
2. Pause and mute during ads; pause when the tab is hidden
3. Check sizing in their iframe and on mobile
4. Hand over a zip ready to upload, plus a list of the changes

My own games have these hooks built in (see my portfolio). Timeline [1–3] days, fixed price $[X]. Is the game already live on the portal or is this the first submission?
```

### E. Playable ad (HTML5 ad for mobile campaigns)
```text
Hi [name], I build HTML5 games that are small, single-file and fast, which is what playable ads need.

For your [app/game], I would recreate the core moment in 15–30 seconds of play, end with a clear call-to-action, and keep it within the ad network's size and format rules for [network].

You get a playable preview to test on your phone in [2] days. Fixed price $[X] for one concept, + $[Y] per extra variation.

Which ad networks are you targeting?
```

### F. Interactive web feature (quiz, calculator, animated landing element)
```text
Hi [name], I can build the [quiz / interactive element] as a lightweight HTML5 component that you embed with one line, works on mobile, and sends results to [your tool].

Plan: wireframe today, working version in [2] days, polish + embed help on day [3]. Fixed price $[X].

Do you have the questions/content ready, or should I draft them?
```

### Screening question answers
- **Describe your recent experience with similar projects:** "I designed and built four HTML5
  products: a branded mini-games kit (catch, memory, prize wheel with coupons and lead capture),
  a physics merge puzzle, a 12-level brick-breaker and an isometric stacking game. All are
  playable in my portfolio."
- **How long will it take?** Always answer with a plan: prototype day X, final day Y.
- **Have you worked with [engine]?** Be honest. If it is not your stack, say what you would use
  instead and why, or skip the job.
