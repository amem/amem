# START HERE — Starfruit Arcade launch kit

You have four finished products, a website, listing images, trailers and every text
you need to open shop. This folder is the plan. Follow it top to bottom.

## The business in one picture

```
            ┌────────────── FAST MONEY (services) ──────────────┐
 Demos  ──▶ │ Fiverr gigs · Upwork proposals · local businesses │ ──▶ first income in days/weeks
 (games)    └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    │       ┌────────────── SLOW MONEY (products) ──────────────┐
    └─────▶ │ itch.io · Gumroad · CodeCanyon · game portals     │ ──▶ passive income that grows
            └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Services pay first.** A business that wants a branded game pays $60–$320 per order.
  The games in `games/` are your portfolio *and* your production engine: most orders are a
  rebrand of a product you already own, delivered in hours, not weeks.
- **Products pay forever.** The same games sell as source code ($15–$179) and earn ad
  revenue on web-game portals. Slow to start, zero extra work per sale.

## The 14-day launch plan

Tick these off in order. Everything you need to paste is in the files above.

### Day 1 — Open shop (about 3 hours)
- [ ] Rename the studio if you want (search "Starfruit Arcade" across the project) — check that the name is free on Fiverr, itch.io and as a domain.
- [ ] Put the website online (01-accounts-setup.md §5). Test every game on your phone.
- [ ] Create the Fiverr seller account, finish the profile, publish **Gig 1 (branded mini-game)** and **Gig 2 (HTML5 game)**.
- [ ] Create the Upwork account and submit the profile for approval.

### Day 2 — Products live
- [ ] itch.io: publish the 4 free browser games and the 4 source-code pages.
- [ ] Gumroad: publish the 4 products and the bundle, then paste the links into `site/site.config.js` and re-deploy the site.
- [ ] Publish **Gig 3 (fixes)** and **Gig 4 (spin the wheel)** on Fiverr.

### Day 3 — Direct outreach (your fastest first sale)
- [ ] Make a list of 20 local shops, cafés, gyms, salons or online stores with an Instagram account.
- [ ] For 5 of them, make a 5-minute rebrand of Spin & Win or Catch Rush (08-order-fulfillment.md §1), record a 15-second screen video, and send the DM template from 07-marketing.md §1. The demo is for them only; do not post their brand publicly.

### Days 4–7 — Proposals and visibility
- [ ] Upwork: send 2–4 tailored proposals per day (03-upwork.md §6). Quality beats volume.
- [ ] Fiverr: be online, answer every message fast, check Briefs daily.
- [ ] Post one trailer per day on TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts (07-marketing.md §3).
- [ ] Post the games on r/WebGames and r/playmygame (read each subreddit's rules first).

### Days 8–14 — Compound
- [ ] Deliver early and over-deliver on the first orders. Ask for honest reviews (never offer anything in exchange).
- [ ] Submit the games to CrazyGames and GameDistribution (06-portals-and-marketplaces.md).
- [ ] Contact 5 more businesses every weekday.
- [ ] After 3–5 good reviews on Fiverr, raise prices by about 20 % (09-pricing-and-growth.md).

## Daily routine once you are running (60–90 minutes)

1. Reply to every Fiverr/Upwork message (speed wins orders).
2. Two Upwork proposals for jobs posted in the last few hours.
3. Five business DMs or emails.
4. One short video post.
5. Work on active orders; deliver before the deadline.

## Keep expectations honest

Nobody can promise sales. New Fiverr sellers often wait days to a few weeks for the first
order; itch.io and Gumroad sales stay small until you have traffic. What reliably moves
the needle: showing a business *its own brand* inside a playable game, fast replies, and
delivering early. Track what you send and what converts (a simple spreadsheet is enough)
and double down on what works.
