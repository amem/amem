Take control of your money in 2027 with one simple spreadsheet that does all the math for you. Budget, bills, savings goals, debt payoff, net worth AND a holiday gift tracker. Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE (11 TABS)
• Start Here: set your year and starting balance, links to every tab
• Categories & Budget: up to 10 income and 30 expense categories + a 50/30/20 check
• Transactions: log every income and expense (1,000 rows, drop-down categories)
• Monthly Dashboard: pick a month and see budget vs. actual, left over, savings rate and 2 charts
• Annual Overview: all 12 months side by side, totals, averages, variance, running balance
• Bills Tracker: due dates, autopay and a Paid/Unpaid check for every month
• Savings Goals: progress bars + how much to save each month to hit your date
• Net Worth Tracker: assets vs. debts, charted across the year
• Debt Payoff Planner: Snowball or Avalanche, payoff date for each debt, total interest, debt-free date
• Debt Schedule: automatic month-by-month plan (up to 30 years)
• BONUS Holiday Planner: gift list, budget per person, gift status, food/decor/travel costs
