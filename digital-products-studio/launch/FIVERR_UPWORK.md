# Fiverr and Upwork kit: sell services built on your products

Fiverr and Upwork sell **services**, not downloads. The strongest offers are **customized versions of the products you already own**: the budget planner, Invoice Studio, Neon Stack and the Python kit. The work is quick for you (the product is done), and every sale is a real, deliverable service.

## Rules that protect your accounts

- **Upwork:** never let a bot or browser automation send proposals or messages. Upwork permanently suspends accounts for it and can hold the funds. AI can *draft*; **you review and click Send** [1][2].
- **Fiverr:** AI-assisted work is allowed in every category. Services that misrepresent who did the work, and fake engagement, are not [3][4].
- Answer clients yourself, only promise what you can deliver, and never buy or fake reviews.
- One account per person on each platform. Complete identity verification yourself.

### Gig 1: Custom budget spreadsheet
- **Title:** I will create a custom budget spreadsheet in Google Sheets or Excel
- **Category:** Data → Data Entry / Spreadsheets (or Business → Financial Consulting → Budgeting)
- **Tags:** budget spreadsheet, google sheets, excel template, personal finance, debt tracker
- **Packages:**
  | | Basic, $25 | Standard, $60 | Premium, $120 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | What | My 2027 budget planner set up with your categories, currency and starting balance | + custom tabs (e.g. business expenses, sinking funds) and dashboard tweaks | Fully custom tracker for your household or small business + a 20-min screen-recorded walkthrough |
  | Delivery | 2 days | 3 days | 5 days |
  | Revisions | 1 | 2 | 3 |
- **Description:**
  > Tired of messy budgets? I'll set up a clean, automatic budget spreadsheet in Google Sheets or Excel that does all the math for you: monthly dashboard, bills, savings goals, debt payoff (snowball or avalanche) and net worth.
  > Every formula is tested, it works in any currency, and you can reuse it every year.
  > Before ordering, message me what you want to track and I'll suggest the right package.
- **Requirements (questions for the buyer):** Google Sheets or Excel? · Currency? · Your income and expense categories · Anything specific to track?

It runs in the browser with no monthly subscription, and the data stays on your computer. I'll brand it for your business and set it up for your country's invoice details.

### Gig 3: HTML5 game reskin or portal integration

## Upwork: profile, project catalog and proposal template

**Profile title:** Spreadsheet Automation & JavaScript Web Tools | Google Sheets · Excel · Python

**Overview:**
> I build practical tools that save small businesses and freelancers hours every week:
> • Automated Google Sheets and Excel trackers: budgets, invoices, inventory, dashboards
> • Lightweight web apps (JavaScript) that run offline, for example invoice and quote generators
> • Python scripts that automate repetitive file, Excel and PDF work
> • Casual HTML5 games for websites and game portals
> Everything I deliver is tested and documented, and built so you can maintain it yourself. Tell me what you do by hand today and I'll show you how to automate it.

**Skills:** Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, JavaScript, HTML5, Python, Data Entry Automation, Financial Modeling (basic), Web Application

**Rate to start:** about $25–35/hour, or fixed-price projects. Raise it after the first reviews.

**Project Catalog** (fixed-price offers clients can buy directly): list the Gig 1, 2 and 4 offers with the same tiers.

**Proposal template** (personalize every one, then send it yourself):
> Hi [name], I read your post about [their exact problem]. I recently built [closest product: a budget planner with 15,000 tested formulas / an offline invoice app / a Python toolkit], so I can deliver [their outcome] by [date].
> My plan: 1) [step] 2) [step] 3) [step]. I'll share a first version by [day] for your feedback.
> Quick question: [one smart question about their data or format]
> Here's a preview of similar work: [link]

Only apply to jobs you can truly deliver; Upwork charges Connects for each proposal.

## Reality check

New Fiverr gigs and Upwork profiles usually take days to weeks to get their first order. What helps most: fast, personal replies, a clear portfolio (your covers and the playable game), low starter prices for the first 3–5 reviews, and delivering early.
